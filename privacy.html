Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

TOPLANE KINGDOM is a Discord community for League of Legends players interested in Toplane and related gameplay.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when using TOPLANE KINGDOM and its related services.

2. Information We Collect

When you use TOPLANE KINGDOM, we may collect information required to provide the service, including your Discord user ID and, when applicable, information associated with your League of Legends account.

This may include publicly available League information such as your Riot ID, region, ranked information, and other game-related information required for the features of the service.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide community features such as account verification, player profiles, rank roles, sparring matchmaking, and other TOPLANE KINGDOM features.

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Discord and Riot Games

TOPLANE KINGDOM uses Discord and may use Riot Games services and APIs to provide its features.

TOPLANE KINGDOM is not affiliated with or endorsed by Riot Games, Inc. or Discord Inc.

5. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the features of TOPLANE KINGDOM or to comply with applicable requirements.

6. Data Removal

Users may request removal of information associated with their TOPLANE KINGDOM account by contacting the server administration.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service changes. The latest version will always be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the TOPLANE KINGDOM administration through the official Discord server.
